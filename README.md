# medicaid-pharmacy-spending-analysis

**Question 2: How did total Mediciad pharmacy reimbursement change from quarter to quarter in 2024?**

- Reimbursement increased in every quarter of 2024, with an overall increase of approximately 20.4% between Q1 and Q4.
- Quarter 3 experienced the greatest quarterly growth. Reimbursement increased by approximately $108.54 million between Q2 and Q3, representing a 13.25% increase.
- Reimbursement increased in Q2 despite declining prescription volume. Prescription volume declined slightly from Q1 to Q2, but reimbursement still increased. This suggests that the volume of prescriptions alone does not explain the increase. Average reimbursement per prescription, filling more high cost or specialty drugs compared to previous periods, may have been contributing factors.
- Q4 had the highest reimbursement and utilization. Quarter 4 recorded approximately $975.80 million in reimbursement and 5.27 million prescriptions.

**Business Recommendation:**
Based on the reported findings, stakeholders should further investigate the types of medications and Medicaid programs contributing to the significant reimbursement increase from quarter 2 to quarter 3. An additional analysis is needed to distinguish the impact of volume driven increases and cost driven increases. Final recommendations can be presented after we can determine if reimbursement per prescription or changes in medication utilization were the contributing factors to the increase. 

**Data limitation:** The analysis excludes records with missing reimbursement or prescription values, including suppressed observations. Results therefore represent the available records included in the analysis.
